TRIBUTE TO HERBERT BAYER

Inspired by Herbert Bayer's Universal Alphabet (1925). Bayer believed in radical simplicity—only lowercase letters, pure geometric forms, and zero unnecessary ornament. His most famous work is the Universal typeface, which stripped the alphabet down to circles, triangles, and lines.

Fun fact: Bayer designed the iconic Bauhaus logo and later became the art director for Vogue magazine. He also lost vision in one eye, which he said made him see design more clearly.
