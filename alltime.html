TRIBUTE TO LÁSZLÓ MOHOLY-NAGY

Inspired by László Moholy-Nagy's Constructivist Compositions (1920s-30s). Moholy-Nagy pioneered the integration of photography, typography, and information design. His most famous work includes the "Light-Space Modulator" kinetic sculpture and his revolutionary book "The New Vision."

Fun fact: Moholy-Nagy once ordered paintings by telephone—he called a sign factory and dictated colors and shapes from a color chart, proving that art could be created without the artist's hand ever touching the canvas.
